Personal details
How to fill this in
  • Target job title: copy the title from the job posting. Data Analyst II Motivated professional seeking opportunities
  • Email: ideally first.last, no nicknames.
  • LinkedIn and portfolio: you can paste the full link; it is cleaned up automatically.
  • Don't include a photo, date of birth, marital status, ID numbers or your full address. They aren't expected on a resume and expose your data.
Professional summary
How to fill this in
  • Use 2 to 4 sentences in this order: who you are + years of experience, main tools, one result with a number and what you are looking for.
  • Data analyst with 4 years of experience in SQL and Power BI. Automated reports that saved 20 hours a month.
  • I am a proactive, dynamic and communicative person. Generic adjectives don't say what you do.
  • No experience yet? Talk about your studies, projects and what you want to learn.
Work experience
How to fill this in
  • Start with your most recent job. Internships, freelance and volunteer work count too.
  • Achievements: past-tense verb + what you did + tool + a measurable result. For your current job, present tense works too ("Develop", "Lead").
  • Reduced customer response time by 30% by rolling out a new Zendesk workflow
  • Responsible for customer service Describes the role, not the result.
  • Don't know the exact number? Use an honest estimate: "about 50 clients a month".
Education
How to fill this in
  • Write the degree type and field: Bachelor of Science in Computer Science BSCS
  • School with its full name and the abbreviation in parentheses.
  • Still studying? Choose "In progress" and enter the expected graduation date.
  • Include high school only if it is your highest level of education.
Skills, languages and certifications
How to fill this in
  • Skills: one category per line, as Category: comma-separated items. The category is shown in bold.
  • Use the same terms as the job posting: if it asks for "Power BI", don't write just "BI".
  • Languages: Language: level. Levels: basic, intermediate, advanced, fluent or native. Be honest; it may be tested in the interview.
  • Certifications: official name and year. Short courses only if they relate to the job.
